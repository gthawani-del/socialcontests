# Arcade Rush
Single-player 90-second score attack; 2 boot flippers, moving targets, goal, combo and multiball. No BANK action. Drain auto-relaunches in 1 second while clock continues; retained score, combo reset. At 0 seconds final score locks.
Proposed points: standard bumper +50, advanced +100, goal +500, perfect kick +150, super-shot goal +300 bonus. Qualified hit chains activate x2-x5 multiplier with timed expiration. Time target +10 seconds, maximum 2 triggers and hard match cap 120 seconds. Multiball 2-3 balls for 10s, one multiball event at a time, unique ball IDs, dedupe hits. Final 15 seconds = visibly announced Rush phase, configurable score bonus (no hidden physics cheat).
Test clock during relaunch, timer cap, combo reset, multiball scoring, Rush transition, pause and result.
